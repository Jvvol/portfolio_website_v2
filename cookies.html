What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small files a website asks your browser to keep. Sites can also keep small settings in your browser’s local storage, which works similarly but is never sent to a server automatically. Privacy laws treat both the same way, so this policy covers both.

What this site uses

This site sets no cookies. It keeps two settings in your browser’s local storage, both strictly necessary for features you use:

Neither setting is ever sent to me or anyone else.

Categories

  • Strictly necessary: needed for something you asked for, such as keeping your theme. Always on. The two items above are the only ones.
  • Functional: extra conveniences, such as embedded players. The Spotify and YouTube players on this site load only when you press “Play here,” and pressing it is how you choose them. Once loaded, those companies may set their own cookies.
  • Analytics: measuring how the site is used. None on this site.
  • Marketing: advertising or tracking across sites. None on this site.

If I ever add anything optional, it won’t run until you agree to it. A banner will ask first, with equally clear options to accept or reject.

Other companies

No other company’s scripts, fonts, or images load on this site until you ask for them. The contact form sends your message to Formspree only when you press Send, and it does so without loading anything from Formspree, so Formspree doesn’t set cookies through this site. If JavaScript is turned off in your browser, sending the form takes you to a Formspree confirmation page, where Formspree’s own policies apply.

Links to other sites, such as Spotify or Instagram, don’t load anything until you follow them.

Embedded players work the same way. Until you press “Play here,” the page shows only a button. Pressing it loads the player from Spotify or YouTube (using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode), and from then on Spotify’s cookie policy or Google’s applies to that player. Reloading the page removes it.

Changing your choices

  • Use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page to review or change your choices at any time.
  • You can delete everything this site has stored using your browser’s settings for site data.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, optional analytics and marketing stay off automatically.
