Accessibility Statement

Effective October 22, 2026

This is a plain-language policy for a personal portfolio site. I want everyone to be able to use it, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom, or other assistive technology.

The standard I aim for

This site aims to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG 2.1, which is the standard most accessibility laws and policies point to.

What I’ve done

  • Headings, landmarks, and lists that give the page a structure screen readers can navigate.
  • A “Skip to content” link as the first thing on every page.
  • Every control works with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus outline.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG AA color contrast in both the light and dark themes.
  • Text can be zoomed to 200% without content being cut off, and the layout adapts down to small phone screens.
  • Images have text alternatives; decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and errors are described in text next to the field, not just by color.
  • Animation is subtle and turns off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Nothing on the site has a time limit, autoplays sound, or flashes.
  • Every page was checked with automated accessibility testing (axe) in both themes and by hand with a keyboard on October 21, 2026.

Known limitations

  • Resume downloads. The PDF and Word versions of my resume haven’t been fully checked for accessibility. The same information is on the home page (experience and education in Resume, projects in Projects, skills in About), and I can send it in another format on request.
  • Other sites. Links to Spotify, Instagram, LinkedIn, GitHub, and itch.io lead to sites I don’t control.
  • Embedded players. The Spotify and YouTube players that load when you press “Play here” are made by those companies, and I can’t change how they work with assistive technology. Each release also links straight to the service.
  • Assistive technology testing. I haven’t yet tested with every screen reader. If something doesn’t work with yours, please tell me.

Compatibility

The site is built with standard HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and is designed to work in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari, along with the assistive technology that works with them. The About and Resume sections, these policy pages, and the contact form work without JavaScript; the Projects and Music lists need it.

Report a problem

If you have trouble using any part of the site, or need content in a different format, email jawolfe17@yahoo.com. Let me know the page and what went wrong. I’ll reply within 5 business days and work with you to fix it or get you the information another way.
