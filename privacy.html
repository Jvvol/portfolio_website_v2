Who is responsible

This site, jvvol.github.io/portfolio_website_v2, is the personal portfolio of Jacob Wolfe. I’m responsible for the personal information described here (under the GDPR and UK GDPR, I’m the “controller”).

Contact: jawolfe17@yahoo.com. A postal address is available on request.

What I collect

When you send a message through the contact form

Your name, email address, and message. The form is processed by Formspree, which also receives the technical details any website receives when you connect to it, such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of submission. Formspree forwards your message to my email inbox.

When you email me directly

Your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write.

When you visit any page

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical information about each visit, including your IP address, for security and to keep the service running. I can’t see or access these logs. GitHub explains how it handles them in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Settings stored on your device

If you switch between light and dark themes, or make a choice in Cookie settings, your browser stores that choice so the site remembers it. These settings stay on your device and are never sent to me. The Cookie Policy lists them.

What I don’t collect

This site has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no social media widgets, no accounts, and no payments. It doesn’t load fonts or scripts from other companies’ servers.

How I use it, and why I’m allowed to

  • To reply to you and continue the conversation you started. Legal basis (GDPR): my legitimate interest in answering messages people choose to send me.
  • To discuss work you ask about, such as a job, internship, or recording, mixing, or mastering session. Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into an agreement.
  • To keep the form free of spam and abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interests.
  • To meet legal obligations, if a law requires me to keep or disclose information. Legal basis: legal obligation.

I don’t use your information for marketing, I don’t add you to any mailing list, and I don’t make automated decisions about you.

Who receives it

I only share your information with the services that make the site and my email work, and only as needed for that:

  • Formspree, Inc. (United States): receives and forwards contact form messages. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States): hosts the site.
  • Yahoo (my email provider): stores the messages in my inbox.

I may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect my rights or someone’s safety.

I do not sell or share your information

I do not sell or share your personal information, as “sell” and “share” are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar US state laws, and I don’t use it for targeted advertising. Because of that, this site doesn’t need a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link. If that ever changes, I’ll update this policy and add the link first.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, the site treats it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information and of any optional analytics or advertising cookies. Today there’s nothing to opt out of, but the signal is honored automatically if that ever changes.

International transfers

I’m based in the United States, and so are the services listed above. If you write to me from another country, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Canada, your information will be processed in the United States. Where these providers offer safeguards for such transfers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, they apply. By sending a message you understand it will be handled in the United States.

How long I keep it

  • Messages (in Formspree and my inbox): while we’re in touch, then deleted within 24 months of our last exchange, unless I need them longer for an ongoing project or a legal reason.
  • Hosting logs: kept by GitHub under its own policies.
  • Settings on your device: until you clear your browser’s site data.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask me to show you, correct, or delete the information I have about you. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights:

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

You have the right to access, correct, and erase your information; to restrict or object to how I use it; to receive it in a portable format; and to withdraw consent where I rely on it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner’s Office in the UK.

California and other US states

Residents of California and other states with privacy laws (including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, and others) may have the right to know what personal information I’ve collected, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale, sharing, or use for targeted advertising (which I don’t do). I won’t treat you differently for using any of these rights.

In the past 12 months, the categories of personal information I may have collected are: identifiers (name, email address, IP address), internet activity (hosting logs kept by GitHub), and the contents of messages you sent me. They came from you, and I used them only for the purposes described above. I have not sold or shared any of them.

Canada

You can ask to access or correct your information and withdraw your consent, and you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

How to make a request

Email jawolfe17@yahoo.com. To confirm the request is really yours, I’ll usually ask you to write from the email address you used to contact me. Someone you authorize can also make a request for you, with proof that you’ve authorized them. I’ll respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law where you live requires it.

Children

This site isn’t directed at children. I don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, email me and I’ll delete it.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, it loads no third-party scripts, and its security settings limit where the page may send data. No system is perfectly secure, though, so please don’t send passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information through the contact form.

Links to other sites

This site links to other services, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Spotify, Instagram, and itch.io. They have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I’ll update the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, I’ll say what changed at the top of this page.
