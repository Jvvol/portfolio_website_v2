About

I’m a computer science senior at West Virginia University with a minor in music technology. I like working on both software and audio, and my favorite projects are the ones that use both.

For software, I mostly write Java and C++, and I’ve built full-stack apps with TypeScript, Node.js, Ruby on Rails, and PostgreSQL. Most of my projects have been team projects run in Agile sprints. One was a 24-hour hackathon, and for another we gave a live demo to a panel of judges. Right now I’m building a web portal with Rails and AWS for my senior capstone.

For music, I use Reaper for my own recording and production. I learned Pro Tools and studio recording in my music tech classes. I scored my friend Owen’s game God Stomp, and I’m writing the soundtrack for his next one, Scratchtastic, which he plans to release on Steam. I also play in three bands: LVNARY, Bulge, and Koal.

My audio plugins combine the two. Etherius is a delay I wrote in C++ with JUCE, based on how analog bucket-brigade delays work, and Granulus is a granular texture processor.

The last two summers I taught programming and robotics to middle and high school students at iD Tech at Carnegie Mellon University. For summer 2027 I’m looking for a software job or internship.
