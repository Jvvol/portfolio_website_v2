          About

          I'm a Computer Science senior at West Virginia University, minoring in Music Technology. I've always enjoyed both the creative and technical sides of making things, and I'm especially drawn to projects where the two overlap.

          On the software side, I work primarily in Java and C++, with full-stack experience using TypeScript, Node.js, Ruby on Rails, and PostgreSQL. I've collaborated on team projects using Agile sprints, built a project in a 24-hour hackathon, and presented a live demo to a panel of judges. Right now, I'm putting those skills to work on my senior capstone: a web portal built with Rails and AWS.

          Music is the other half of what I do. I record and produce my own work in Reaper and have studied Pro Tools and studio recording through my music technology courses. I composed the soundtrack for my friend Owen's game, God Stomp, and I'm working on the music for his next game, Scratch Fever, which he plans to release on Steam. Outside of that, I play in LVNARY, Bulge, and Koal and built the website for LVNARY.

          I bring those interests together through audio software, too. Etherius is a C++ audio delay plugin built with JUCE and inspired by analog bucket-brigade delay circuits. I'm also developing Granulus, a granular texture processor designed to explore the more experimental side of sound.

          I've also spent the last two summers teaching programming and robotics to middle and high school students at iD Tech at Carnegie Mellon University. It's been a great way to share what I know, help students work through problems, and see them bring their own ideas to life.
